Independent boutique hotels (20–180 rooms) in dense micro-markets like **South Beach** and **Mid-Beach** lose **$45,000 to $140,000+ per year** from two silent leaks:
1. **Wholesale & OTA Rate Parity Undercuts**: Wholesalers (via Agoda, Vio, Trip.com, or Booking.com mobile/geo promos) publicly undercut the hotel's own website BAR by **4%–12%**.
